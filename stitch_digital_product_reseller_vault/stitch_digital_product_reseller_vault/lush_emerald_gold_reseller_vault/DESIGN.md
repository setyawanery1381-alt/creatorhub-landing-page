## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-conversion, premium, and trustworthy directory experience for digital asset access and reseller networks. It addresses entrepreneurs, digital marketers, educators, and content creators looking for frictionless access to commercial-grade digital templates, worksheets, and e-learning resources.

The emotional signature is grounded in wealth, abundance, and verified credibility:
- **Authority & Prosperity:** Deep evergreen and botanical pine layers evoke institutional trust, longevity, and high value.
- **Warmth & Commercial Opportunity:** Liquid amber gold accents provide an optimistic, high-yield investment feel.
- **Clarity & Accessibility:** Pristine bright surface cards ensure maximum legibility for fast asset discovery, immediate downloading, and reseller syndication.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from **High-Contrast Modern Bento** and **Curated Linktree-Style Directory Hubs**:
- High-contrast card surfaces layered over an atmospheric multi-point linear gradient (`#013827` to `#9e7d05`).
- Structural arch detailing on top-level headers framed by precise metallic gold contours.
- Energetic color-coded disc signifiers across content taxonomy to make multi-category libraries immediately scannable.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an evocative environmental gradient paired with crisp optical white surface modules:

- **Environmental Canvas:** A diagonal linear gradient (`145deg`) moving through deep forest green (`#013827`), emerald shadows (`#064e3b`), olive brass (`#3f5509`), and culminating in warm antique amber (`#9e7d05`).
- **Brand Accents:**
  - **Emerald Green (`#10B981`):** The primary interactive driver. Communicates action, confirmation, and instant digital delivery.
  - **Gold Border (`#EAB308`):** Serves as the luxury divider and outline for the arched header and hero milestones.
  - **Reseller Glow (`#EA580C` to `#F97316`):** Dual-stop energetic orange gradient reserved for high-intent resale tags ("100% Bisa Dijual Kembali").
  - **Mint Tag (`#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` foreground):** Commercial validation pills indicating resale rights without overpowering interactive buttons.
- **Card Surfaces:** Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with balanced dark text tones (`#0F172A`), generating uncompromising contrast against the dark chromatic background.
- **Category Disc Palette:** Vibrant saturated hues (teal, pink, royal blue, orange, emerald, purple, sky blue, red) assigned strictly to iconography discs to enable rapid cognitive categorization across cards.

## Typography

The type system balances commanding punch with analytical clarity by pairing **Outfit** for structural headings and badges with **Inter** for readable narrative and instructional copy:

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit 700–800):** Rendered in uppercase or assertive title case. Used for brand declarations, hub section headers, and card titles. It features open geometric apertures and high stroke weight to hold authority against rich colored canvas backdrops.
- **Body & Metadata (Inter 400–500):** Delivers clean readability for product descriptions, license explanations, and utility text within bright cards.
- **Badges & Microcopy (Outfit 700 & Inter 600):** Letter-spaced slightly wider (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to maintain sharp legibility at reduced sizes across pill indicators and button states.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected as an adaptable, single-column to responsive multi-column hub:

- **Container Model:** The outer layout uses a fixed-max-width approach constrained to `768px` for ultra-focused linktree-style navigation or expanding to `1120px` for multi-column bento grids.
- **Arched Header Shell:** The top masthead is encased in a deep pine container (`#01291D`) featuring an inverted arched bottom border (`border-bottom-left-radius: 2.5rem; border-bottom-right-radius: 2.5rem`) framed by a crisp gold stroke (`2px solid #EAB308`).
- **Card Bento Grid:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 3-column responsive layout or 2-column feature blocks with `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 2-column balanced flow.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single vertical stream with `0.75rem` vertical gap for optimal touch scrolling and one-thumb navigation.
- **Inner Component Rhythm:** Cards maintain strict internal padding of `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` to prevent visual collision between icon discs, title stacks, and call-to-action triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through high-contrast tonal layering rather than heavy ambient blur:

- **Level 0 (Environment Canvas):** Deep, non-reflective multi-stop gradient background (`#013827` to `#9e7d05`).
- **Level 1 (Arched Anchor Shell):** Solid pine `#01291D` with gold border framing (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Level 2 (Pristine Cards):** Opaque white `#FFFFFF` cards elevated with crisp, layered drop shadows:
  - Default: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`.
  - Hover / Active: `0 20px 35px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.25)`, combined with a `translateY(-3px)` dynamic lift.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Controls & Badges):**
  - Icon discs feature inward crispness and a light soft drop (`0 4px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`).
  - Floating WhatsApp anchor button maintains high elevation: `0 12px 30px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design uses generous, organic roundedness that softens the high contrast between white cards and deep green surfaces:

- **Cards:** Rounded to `1rem` (`rounded-2xl` equivalent) for an approachable, app-like finish.
- **Header Masthead:** Features large custom bottom-corner radials of `2.5rem` (`40px`), forming a distinctive signature arch.
- **Badges & Pills:** Complete pill-shape (`9999px`) for resale rights chips, search bars, and category filter chips.
- **Category Icon Discs:** Perfect circles (`50%` radius, `48px x 48px` or `56px x 56px`) housing centered vector glyphs.
- **Buttons:** Smooth `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`) to `9999px` (pill) depending on priority.

## Components

### 1. Arched Masthead & Header
- **Container:** Background `#01291D`, bordered along the bottom with `#EAB308` (`2px`).
- **Title Stack:** Uppercase main title set in Outfit Bold with warm white fill (`#FFFFFF`). Subheading in muted mint-white (`#A7F3D0`).
- **Reseller Super-Badge:** Pill container with linear gradient background (`#EA580C` to `#F97316`), containing bold uppercase text "100% BISA DIJUAL KEMBALI", gold/white micro-icon, and subtle outer glow (`0 0 16px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.45)`).

### 2. Product Access Card
- **Surface:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), border radius `1rem` (`16px`), padding `1.25rem`.
- **Icon Disc:** `48px` circular container colored by product category (e.g., Teal for E-Books, Pink for Worksheets, Royal Blue for Coding, Emerald for Bundles). Centered icon rendered in pure white.
- **License Tag:** Embedded mint tag pill (`background: #ECFDF5; color: #047857; font-weight: 600; font-size: 11px`), displaying "100% Hak Jual" alongside a checkmark badge.
- **Card Actions:** Secondary subtle button or full-card anchor trigger with an active hover state featuring chevron transitions (`translateX(4px)`).

### 3. Category Filter Chips
- **Inactive:** Semi-transparent dark green pill (`rgba(1, 41, 29, 0.6)`) with subtle border (`1px solid rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.25)`), white text (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Active:** Solid gold `#EAB308` or emerald `#10B981` pill with bold contrasting dark pine text (`#01291D`).

### 4. Search & Discovery Input
- **Container:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, border `1.5px solid rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.4)`.
- **Typography:** `14px` Inter, placeholder in `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to solid `#10B981` with ring shadow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`).

### 5. Primary Action Buttons (CTAs)
- **Primary Access CTA:** Solid emerald `#10B981` fill with white text, font Outfit Bold (`14px`), pill or `12px` rounded corners.
- **Hover:** Brightness boost to `#059669` with `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.

### 6. Floating Consultation Anchor
- **Fixed Button:** Positioned bottom-right (`bottom: 1.5rem; right: 1.5rem`).
- **Visuals:** Emerald `#10B981` circular disc (`56px x 56px`) with white WhatsApp glyph, elevated with persistent pulse ring indicator for real-time reseller support.
